HOW LIFELINK WORKS

A faster way to ask for help.

The platform is designed to reduce confusion during emergencies by bringing the most important actions into one place.

A PEOPLE-FIRST ROADMAP

Useful next steps, built with care.

These are future ideas, not live services. Each would need trusted partners, consent, privacy safeguards, and medical review.

Planned with blood-bank partners

Current stock, clearly dated

Show partner-confirmed availability with a “last updated” time, then help hospitals coordinate stock before usable units expire.

Designed for low connectivity

Language and low-network access

Offer simple local-language guidance and low-data or SMS updates, with a clear opt-in before any message is sent.

Reviewed by qualified professionals

Donor care, not guesswork

Offer optional donation reminders and clear referral guidance. Eligibility decisions stay with licensed staff; the app should never diagnose or approve donation.

ABOUT THIS PROJECT

A prototype for emergency coordination.

LifeLink is currently a front-end demonstration. Requests and donor registrations are stored in this browser only. The project does not verify hospitals or donors, send notifications, or guarantee blood availability. For an actual emergency, contact a hospital or licensed blood bank.
