URGENT HELP

For a medical emergency, contact professionals now.

Call the treating hospital or its blood centre. India’s 112 service is the pan-India emergency response number for urgent assistance.

CLEAR ABOUT TRUST

What LifeLink does—and does not do.

LifeLink is an independent information guide, not a Government of India service, hospital, or blood bank. We help people reach official resources.

  • No patient requests or donor details are collected on this site.
  • Blood availability and donor eligibility must be confirmed by a licensed blood centre and the treating medical team.
  • When you open another service, that service’s privacy policy and terms apply.
